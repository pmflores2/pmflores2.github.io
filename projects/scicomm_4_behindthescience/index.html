Behind The Science Podcast

guest episode

Behind The Science Podcast is hosted by Paul Caesar M. Flores and goes beyond published research papers to tell the stories behind scientific discoveries. Its episodes cover research from across the Philippines, including climate change and disaster resilience, marine science and coral restoration, and agriculture. Paul is my twin brother, and I joined him for two episodes.

Ep106 - Quantum violation of free-fall motion

Released January 22, 2026. What happens when you drop two quantum particles of different sizes at the same time?

Reference: Flores PCM, Galapon EA, Quantum free-fall motion and quantum violation of the weak equivalence principle, Physical Review A 99, 042113 (2019).

Ep85 - A theoretical framework to discriminate chiral molecules

Released September 11, 2025. A conversation with my twin brother Paul about a method to discriminate chiral molecules by shining a laser on them and measuring the spin of the ejected electron.

Reference: Flores PCM, Carlström S, Patchkovskii S, Ivanov M, Mujica V, Ordonez AF, Smirnova O, arXiv:2505.22433 (2025).
