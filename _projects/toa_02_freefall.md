      Key insights
    </div>
    <ul style="margin:0; padding-left:1.1rem;">
      <li>We construct a TOA-operator for a nonrelativistic and structureless particle that is projected upward in a uniform gravitational field with an intended arrival point below the classical turning point</li>
      <li>The expectation value of the TOA-operator is equal to the classical time of arrival plus mass-dependent quantum correction terms in powers of ℏ.</li>
      <li>The TOA distribution exhibits mass-dependence.</li>
    </ul>
