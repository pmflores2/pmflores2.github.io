The KoolPals is a Filipino comedy podcast and live entertainment group that riffs on pop culture, current events and everyday life. It was founded in 2019 by a group of Filipino comedians, and in January 2025 they opened a hub for live podcast recordings and stand-up shows. In 2026, Spotify gave the show a Creator Milestone Award for building a loyal, returning audience.
