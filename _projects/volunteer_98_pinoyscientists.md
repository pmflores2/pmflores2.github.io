<a href="https://pinoyscientists.com/">Pinoy Scientists</a> is an online platform that gives a voice to scientists from the Philippines, with the tagline "Yes, we exist!" It profiles Filipino scientists at home and abroad, publishes longer features on topics like conservation and AI, runs a newsletter, and invites scientists to contribute their own stories. Its day-to-day storytelling happens on <a href="https://facebook.com/pinoyscientists">Facebook</a> and <a href="https://instagram.com/pinoyscientists">Instagram</a>, where it hands the page over to a different scientist each week in a "takeover." The aim is to give young Filipinos role models who look like them and to show that a career in science is possible.
